### Last 3 Months

- 📡 **[UDP777](https://www.udp7777.com/)** – A simple UDP pager in Go *(~2 days)*
- 🔐 **[FIPSPad](https://fipspad.browserbox.io)** – Notepad with encryption-at-rest that only runs when your system is FIPS-compliant, in Rust *(~2 days)*
- 📰 **[HN Front Pages 2035](https://dosaygo-studio.github.io/hn-front-page-2035/)** – A suite of AI-written alternative Hacker News front pages: serious joy and laughs *(10–15 min each, ~1–2 hrs total)*
- 📚 **[HackerBook](https://hackerbook.dosaygo.com)** – Full auto-updating static interactive archive of all of Hacker News with new views and queries *(~4 days)*
- 🔗 **[Hacker Backlinks](https://hacker-backlinks.browserbox.io/)** – "Most cited" HN-ecosystem topic ranking: which discussions get cited most by other HN comments *(~3–4 hours)*
- 🧮 **[HexFiend](https://do-say-go.github.io/hexfiend/?hn=2)** – A game where you do mental math for hexadecimal crossword puzzles *(~1 day)*
- 🔑 **[Insights / RSA Game](https://do-say-go.github.io/insights/)** – Factor RSA semiprimes by doing constraint propagation on a guessable tableaux *(~6 days)*
- ☁️ **[BlueDot](https://tui.bluedot.ink)** – Universal cloud-console TUI: search, compare, and rent VPS across GCP, Hetzner, AWS, Azure, DigitalOcean, and Vultr from your terminal *(~4 days)*
- 🗜️ **[LZW-X](https://github.com/BrowserBox/LZW-X)** – An approximate-matching LZW compression algorithm — an idea from 2013, finally coded *(weeks of thinking over years + ~2 days of coding)*
- 📧 **[ai-chat.email](https://ai-chat.email)** – Email bridge for CLI AI agents: drive your agents from email while you're away, laptop at home *(~12 days so far)*
- 🎙️ **[NoSpy](https://github.com/BrowserBox/NoSpy)** – Open-source macOS tool to mute your mic to zero + detect Siri listening overrides *(~1 hour)*
- 🧹 **[ai-lint](https://ai-lint.dosaygo.com)** – AI "taste & doctrine" files to teach your agents code that belongs: quality checks for semantics, not just syntax *(~3.5 hours)*
- 🔢 **[git-prime](https://textonly.github.io/git-prime/)** – Mine git commit hashes for large primes by fuzzing a nonce annotation in the message *(~1 hour)*
- 📰 **[Prime News](https://dosaygo-studio.github.io/prime-news/)** – A view of Hacker News that only shows items whose ID is a prime number *(~2 days)*
- 📐 **[Pythagorean Proof](https://do-say-go.github.io/insights/)** – Interactive visual proof of the Pythagorean theorem: play with the squares-to-triangles-to-bigger-square mapping *(~1 day)*
- 🖥️ **Windows 98½** – Impressionistic Windows 98 desktop with real web browsers: retro UI wrapper around BrowserBox using the new BrowserBox WebView Embedding API. Corporate showcase meets 90s nostalgia art *(5–6 weeks for the desktop; 7 years for BrowserBox)*
- 📊 **[Structropy](https://github.com/DOSAYGO-STUDIO/structropy)** – Towards a metric of organization: adapts Shannon entropy toward structure, not just surprise. Loosely based on expected sorting time of new inserts *(months of thinking over years + ~1–2 days of coding)*
